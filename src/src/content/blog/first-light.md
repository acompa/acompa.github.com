Welcome to the freshly minted acompa blog! 🚀

This space brings together everything we have been publishing across static HTML pages and notebooks into a single, maintainable Astro project.

## What to expect

- **Engineering and product notes.** Architecture write-ups, experimentation logs, and thoughtful reviews of tools we rely on.
- **Company updates.** Highlights from product launches, conference talks, and hiring news.
- **Deep dives.** The occasional long-form exploration of design systems, machine learning, or analytics work.

We are excited about the portability Astro gives us, especially the ability to ship completely static builds that keep this site fast and friendly to deploy anywhere.

Stay tuned for much more!
